T6 · SPRINT PLANNING CHECKLIST

Pre, during and post sprint planning checklist for BAs.

Pre-Sprint
  • All User Stories in backlog are INVEST compliant
  • Acceptance Criteria written in Gherkin format
  • Stories estimated with Story Points
  • Dependencies identified and linked in JIRA
  • Sprint Goal clearly defined
  • Stakeholders confirmed priorities
During Planning
  • Walk through AC for each story
  • Answer developer questions about requirements
  • Update JIRA: move committed stories to sprint
